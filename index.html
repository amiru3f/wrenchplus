Important calculation notes

Customer invoices include VAT; revenue excludes VAT. The default Dutch VAT assumption is 21%. Operating surplus is not take-home pay or taxable profit.

The model excludes business loans, startup investments, equipment purchase costs, depreciation beyond modeled reserves, unexpected repairs and income tax. The owner's compensation target is shown separately, not treated as a deductible wage expense for a sole proprietor.

Transport covers local parts collection, fuel, parking and occasional deliveries. For a dedicated vehicle, increase this cost to include ownership/lease, insurance, tax and maintenance. Insurance and regulatory costs need actual quotes.
